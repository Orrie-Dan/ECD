# Igitabo cy’ukoresha — Umurezi (Caretaker)

**Sisitemu:** Sisitemu y’Ubwitabire bw’Abana  
**Ururimi:** Ikinyarwanda  
**Igikoresho:** Telefoni / tableti  
**Inshingano:** Umurezi w’ikigo cya ECD

Iri gitabo gisobanura ibyo umurezi akora buri munsi. Ntabwo usabwa kumenya seriveri cyangwa “sync”.

---

## 1. Kwinjira

1. Fungura sisitemu.  
2. Hitamo **Umurezi**.  
3. Andika izina n’ijambo ry’ibanga wyoherejweho.  
4. Ujya ku **Ahabanza**.

Niba umurongo udahari ariko wigeze winjira kandi amakuru yabitswe ku gikoresho, ushobora gukomeza gukora. Niba nta makuru yabitswe, sisitemu izakubwira **Nta makuru yabitswe kuri iki gikoresho** — huza interneti rimwe.

---

## 2. Ahabanza (Dashboard)

Ubona:

- Abana bose  
- Abaje uyu munsi  
- Abataraza  

Ibikorwa by’ibanze:

- **Andika Ubwitabire** — ikintu cya mbere ku munsi  
- **Andikisha Umwana**  
- **Reba urutonde rw’abana**

Hasi hari menu: **Ahabanza · Abana · Ubwitabire · Imikurire · Ibindi**.

---

## 3. Kwiyandikisha umwana

**Ibindi → Andikisha Umwana** cyangwa **Ahabanza → Andikisha Umwana**.

Intambwe 4:

1. **Amakuru y’umwana** — amazina, itariki y’amavuko, igitsina, ikibazo cyihariye (niba gihari).  
2. **Ababyeyi** — umubyeyi wa mbere (amazina, telefoni, isano). Wa kabiri ni uw’ubushake.  
3. **Aho atuye** — Intara → Akarere → Umurenge → Akagari → Umudugudu.  
4. **Genzura no kubika** — reba, hanyuma **Bika Amakuru**.

Ubutumwa **Byabitswe kuri iki gikoresho** = byabitswe ku tableti. Bizajya ku seriveri iyo umurongo ugaruye.

**Guhindura:** Abana → hitamo umwana → **Hindura**.  
Guhindura itariki y’amavuko, igitsina, cyangwa ahantu atuye **bisaba umurongo**.

---

## 4. Ubwitabire (buri munsi)

1. Kanda **Ubwitabire**.  
2. Ku buri mwana hitamo **Yaje** cyangwa **Ntiyaje** — ntibyiyandika ubwabyo.  
3. Niba **Yaje:** andika **Yazanywe na** n’igihe yaje (niba biboneka).  
4. Niba **Ntiyaje:** hitamo **impamvu**.  
5. Bika.

**Raporo:** Ibindi → Raporo — reba abaje / abataje ku ntera y’amatariki.

---

## 5. Imikurire (gupima)

Menu: **Imikurire**.

- Reba abana **bagiye gupimwa**, **batereranye**, cyangwa **mu kaga cy’imirire**.  
- Hitamo umwana → **Andika ipimo**.  
- Andika **ibiro** na **MUAC** (cm).  
- Sisitemu igaragaza: bisanzwe / mu kaga / bikabije / bikabije cyane.

Urutonde rw’ukwezi: **Imikurire → Ukwezi** (nk’ifishi ya Form VII).

**Ubuziranuge:** pima buri mezi. Sisitemu ivuga “igihe cyo gupima” nyuma y’iminsi 30, “batereranye” nyuma y’iminsi 45.

---

## 6. Imirire (ibyo bagaburiwe)

**Ibindi → Imirire**.

Ku munsi, andika niba habaye:

- **Amata**  
- **Igikoma**  
- **Ifunguro (indyo yuzuye)** — iyo uyanditse, kanda ibitsina 6 (ibinyampeke, ibinyamisogwe, amata, inyama/amagi, imboga/imbuto, amavuta).

**Raporo y’ukwezi:** andika litiro z’amata, ifu (kg), n’aho ibiryo byavuye.

Ibi ni ibyo **ikigo** cyagabuye — si umusanzu w’umubyeyi ku giti cye.

---

## 7. STED — gutahura ubumuga hakiri kare

**Ibindi → Gutahura ubumuga**.

Ku mwana ufite **imyaka 1–6**:

1. Hitamo umwana.  
2. Emeza **uruhushya** rw’umubyeyi.  
3. Itegereze **ibice 9 by’umubiri** (umutwe, ijosi, amaboko, …).  
4. Emeza ikigero cy’imyaka (1–3 cyangwa 4–6).  
5. Subiza **YEGO / OYA** ku bibazo byose.  
6. Reba umwanzuro (nta kibazo, inama, gusubiramo mu mezi 6).  
7. **Ohereza isuzuma**.

---

## 8. Guhuza n’umurongo (offline)

Hasi cyangwa mu **Igenamiterere** ureba imiterere:

| Ubutumwa | Icyo bisobanura |
|---|---|
| Uri ku murongo | Hari interneti |
| Nta murongo | Kora; byabikwa ku gikoresho |
| Birimo guhuza… | Birimo guhuzwa |
| Impinduka N zitegereje | Hari byabitswe bitarageze kuri seriveri |
| Byahujwe … | Byageze kuri seriveri |
| Guhuza byanze | Amakuru agumye ku gikoresho — ongera ugerageze |
| Birakeneye kwitabwaho | Hari amakuru ahuriye — seriveri ni yo ikoreshwa |

**Huza ubu** iyo ushaka guhuza nonaha.

### Gusohoka

Niba hari impinduka zitarahujwe:

1. **Huza hanyuma usohoke** — byiza  
2. **Bika ku gikoresho** — uzongera kubona amakuru yawe  
3. **Siba amakuru yo ku gikoresho** — birasiba ibitaroherejwe (ntibisubizwa)

---

## 9. Igenamiterere

- Izina ry’ikigo (reba)  
- Amazina yawe na telefoni  
- Imiterere yo **guhuza** (pending / failed / conflict)

---

## 11. Inama ngufi

- Andika **ubwitabire buri munsi** mbere y’amasaha y’amafaranga.  
- Ntuzigere uhindura tableti nta PIN / ntuzimye umuntu undi akoresha konti yawe.  
- Iyo tableti yatakaye, menyesha ubuyobozi **ako kanya**.  
- Ntukore kuri mudasobwa rusange.
